1. Set the ownership standard

Define the decision owner, support roles, backup coverage, service expectation, escalation path, and source of truth for each workflow.

One accountable owner

Name the person who makes the decision and confirms the process is complete. A department name alone is not enough.

Backup before volume

Assign backup coverage before absences, system outages, carrier delays, or lead spikes create a live gap.

Document the handoff

State what arrives, what must be recorded, what moves next, and when leadership should be alerted.
